How to Prepare for an Interview

Getting ready for an interview starts with learning about the job and the company. Read the job description and visit the company's website so you understand what it does and what skills it needs. Think about a few examples from school, work, or volunteering that show those skills. Review your resume too, and be ready to explain the experience and projects you included.

Practicing out loud can help you feel more comfortable when it is time to answer questions. Ask a friend to do a practice interview and give you feedback. Prepare questions about the job so you can decide whether it is a good fit for you. Plan your outfit and travel ahead of time, or check your camera, microphone, and internet connection for an online interview. Taking care of these details gives you more time to focus on the conversation.

Your interview checklist

  • Research the employer and review the skills listed in the job description.
  • Practice common questions out loud, including a short introduction about yourself.
  • Prepare specific examples of teamwork, problem-solving, and other relevant skills.
  • Review your resume and bring extra copies to an in-person interview.
  • Write down a few questions about the role, team, and expectations.
  • Choose appropriate clothes and plan to arrive 10 to 15 minutes early.
  • For a virtual interview, test your equipment and choose a quiet space.
Advice adapted from UC Riverside Career Center and CareerOneStop.
